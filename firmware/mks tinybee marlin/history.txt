2026-05-30: User asked about endstop wiring (X-Stop, Y-Stop) for MKS TinyBee.
- Board pinout: X_STOP_PIN=IO33, Y_STOP_PIN=IO32, Z_STOP_PIN=IO22.
- Connector has 3 pins: GND, +5V, Signal.
- Marlin Configuration.h: ENDSTOPPULLUPS is ENABLED (line 794).
- Marlin Configuration.h: X_MIN_ENDSTOP_INVERTING=true, Y_MIN_ENDSTOP_INVERTING=true (lines 832-833).
- With ENDSTOPPULLUPS enabled, the ESP32 GPIO pin uses internal pull-up resistor (~45kΩ to 3.3V).
- Signal is normally HIGH (3.3V) when endstop is NOT triggered.
- When endstop is triggered, Signal must connect to GND to pull the pin LOW.
- This matches the standard "Normally Open (NO), Signal connects to GND" configuration.
- The +5V pin on the connector is NOT used for mechanical endstops — it's for optional sensors.
- Inverting=true means: triggered = LOW (pin reads LOW when switch is pressed).
